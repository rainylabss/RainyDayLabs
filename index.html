🍪 Chocolate Chunk Cookies

Ingredients

  • 100g butter
  • 100g light brown sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 150g plain flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 100g chocolate chunks

👩‍🍳 Steps

  1. Cream the softened butter and brown sugar together.
  2. Add the egg and mix until combined.
  3. Add the flour and baking powder. Mix until you have a dough.
  4. Stir in the chocolate chunks.
  5. Shape the dough into cookie balls and place them on a lined baking tray.
  6. Bake at 180°C until golden around the edges. Ask an adult to help with the oven.
  7. Let the cookies cool before eating.

🧁 Easy Cupcakes

Ingredients

  • 100g butter
  • 100g sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 100g self-raising flour
  • 1 tsp vanilla

👩‍🍳 Steps

  1. Beat the butter and sugar together.
  2. Add the eggs one at a time.
  3. Fold in the flour.
  4. Add vanilla and mix gently.
  5. Spoon the mixture into cupcake cases.
  6. Bake until risen and golden. Ask an adult to help with the oven.
  7. Cool completely before decorating.

🍫 Chocolate Brownies

Ingredients

  • Butter
  • Sugar
  • Eggs
  • Cocoa powder
  • Plain flour
  • Chocolate

👩‍🍳 Steps

  1. Melt the butter and chocolate together.
  2. Mix in the sugar.
  3. Add the eggs and mix well.
  4. Fold in flour and cocoa powder.
  5. Pour into a lined baking tin.
  6. Bake until the edges are set and the middle is slightly soft. Ask an adult to help with the oven.
  7. Cool before slicing.
